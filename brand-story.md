--------------------------------------------------
BRAND STORY — FROM HORSE TO TRUCK
--------------------------------------------------

A central creative concept of GÖKTÜRK LOJİSTİK is the evolution of transportation.

Historically, horses were deeply connected with Turkic peoples, representing:
- movement
- freedom
- endurance
- speed
- exploration
- long-distance journeys

Today, the modern equivalent of that long-distance journey is the truck.

The brand should establish a subtle visual relationship between:

HORSE → JOURNEY → ROAD → TRUCK

Core brand statement:

"YOLCULUK DEĞİŞTİ. RUH AYNI."

Do NOT present the horse as a random decorative Turkish symbol.

Instead, integrate the concept into the storytelling and motion design of the website.

Possible visual transitions:

- A running horse silhouette gradually transforms into a moving truck.
- Horse hoofprints transform into road markings.
- A traditional route/path transforms into a modern logistics route.
- Horse movement inspires the motion of the animated truck marker on the map.
- A horse running across a steppe can transition into a modern truck travelling across a highway.
- Historical journey → modern transportation.

The horse should appear selectively and elegantly.

Do NOT use:
- cartoon horses
- fantasy imagery
- excessive Ottoman/Turkish ornaments
- cliché patriotic imagery
- overly literal historical scenes

The visual language should remain premium, cinematic and contemporary.

The relationship should communicate:

"Transportation has evolved.
The spirit of the journey remains."

This concept should become one of the defining visual identities of GÖKTÜRK LOJİSTİK.
